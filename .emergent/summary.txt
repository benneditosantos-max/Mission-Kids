<product_requirements>
The MissionKids WebApp is a responsive, gamified PWA designed for family task and finance management, targeting two children (13 and 11 years old). It aims to transform household/school routines into fun missions, rewarding responsibility with XP, virtual coins, and real allowance, fostering organization, discipline, and financial literacy.

Key features include:
-   **User Profiles**: Distinct Parents (administrative panel, task CRUD, assignment of monetary/XP values, frequency, timers, parental validation, alerts, allowance/savings management, historical data) and Children (gamified dashboard with To Do/In Progress/Completed sections, allowance progress bar, customizable avatar with level/XP, configurable sounds, photo proof upload, bonus missions, internal XP store).
-   **Financial System**: Progressive allowance tied to task completion, up to 3 savings goals per child (bike, game), graphical progress visualization, and parent-controlled payment/reset.
-   **Gamification**: XP and leveling system for avatars, personalization store, badges/achievements, timed tasks, bonus missions.
-   **Notifications**: Personalized alerts for children (task reminders, overdue, completion sounds) and parents (task completion, approval needed, with distinct sounds).
-   **UI/UX**: Modern, kid-friendly design with a specific color palette (#4F46E5, #FBBF24, #22C55E, #F9FAFB), Nunito font, responsive layout, cartoon avatars, and micro-interactions.
-   **Auth/Backend Evolution**: Initially set up with MongoDB/FastAPI and custom email/password/PIN authentication. Later, a major pivot was requested to Firebase Authentication for parents, custom username/PIN for children (stored in Firestore as sub-accounts linked to ), and Firebase Firestore for data storage, including Cloud Functions for automation. Image storage via Firebase Storage.
</product_requirements>
